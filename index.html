How it works

Protein % = protein per serving ÷ serving size × 100.

Gluten to add = ((target % − current %) ÷ 100) ÷ (0.75 − current % ÷ 100) × target amount. Flour needed = target amount − gluten. Vital wheat gluten is treated as 75% protein.
